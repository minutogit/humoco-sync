## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S03-12` — Zero-Value Semantics for `media.max_user_count` and `media.max_channel_mb` (`rust/src/db.rs:1239, 1256` vs `php/sync.php:1590, 1593`)**
  - **Problem:** `SyncDb::put_media` checks `max_user_count > 0 && ...` and `if max_channel_bytes > 0`, treating `0` as unlimited/disabled limit, whereas `php/sync.php` rejects `max_user_count < 1` or `newSize > maxChannelBytes` (`0`) with `507 Insufficient Storage`. (`SPEC.md` §3 documents `ttl_days = 0` as unlimited/no expiry but does not specify whether `0` on `max_user_count` or `max_channel_mb` means `0` capacity or unlimited.)
  - **Options:**
    1. Align Rust and `Config::validate()` with PHP so `0` rejects media uploads (`507`) or fails config validation (`>= 1`).
    2. Align PHP to Rust (`0` = unlimited) and document the sentinel in `SPEC.md` §3.
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1** (`>= 1` validation or `507` on `0`, matching PHP); left untouched without guessing operator/spec intent.
