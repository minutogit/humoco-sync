## Implemented Safe Fixes

1. **Cluster 1 — Enforce `cfg.lock.max_buckets` on Ticket Issuance & New Bucket Creation (`rust/src/locks.rs`)**
   - Added `redb::ReadableTableMetadata` import (`rust/src/locks.rs:10`).
   - Updated `SyncDb::issue_genesis_ticket` (`rust/src/locks.rs:717-743`) to return `507 Lock bucket budget exhausted` when the derived bucket does not yet exist and `buckets.len()? >= cfg.lock.max_buckets` (while allowing tickets for already-existing buckets).
   - Updated `SyncDb::execute_lock_batch_in_txn` Step 3 (`rust/src/locks.rs:1093-1096`) to reject new bucket creation (`existing_bucket == None`) with `507 Lock bucket budget exhausted` when `buckets.len()? >= cfg.lock.max_buckets` (preserving Step 1 rollback `SelfFrozen` tombstone creation at `lines 922-948` and writes to existing active buckets).
   - Regression tests: `locks::tests::test_s01_max_buckets_does_not_suppress_rollback_self_freeze_tombstone` (`rust/src/locks.rs:1563-1654`) and `test_s01_max_buckets_enforced_on_ticket_and_batch_creation` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs:8942-9113`).

2. **Cluster 2 — Reject Whitespace/Non-Hex in `RevocationNotice::signature` (`rust/src/crypto.rs`)**
   - Updated `verify_revocation_notice` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:369-371`) to check `is_hex_exact(&notice.signature.to_ascii_lowercase(), 128)` before `verify_ed25519_hex`, eliminating `.trim()` acceptance of trailing newlines/spaces in `PUT ?revoke` while preserving uppercase-to-lowercase normalization.
   - Regression tests: Case G in `crypto::tests::test_s01_verify_revocation_notice_rejects_delimiter_injection_and_bad_urls` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:934-951`) and trailing-newline `PUT ?revoke` check in `test_audit_decisions_cluster4_revoke_and_delete_account_parity` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs:8734-8752`).

## Cross-Slice Follow-Up Notes *(Out of `S01` Scope)*
Observed during cross-module / PHP parity inspection; left untouched in `S01` for their respective slice/flow audits:
- **`S02` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:1038-1044, 1112`):** `process_lock_import` checks `max_inodes` when creating a previously absent bucket (`buckets.get(bucket_hex.as_str())?.is_none()`), without enforcing `cfg.lock.max_buckets`. Also (`S02`/`S09-F3`), `process_lock_import` (`lines 1046-1067`) recomputes `bucket_head` in `BTreeMap` `slot` order rather than insertion order; if a server retained across a rotation receives a pre-rotation `known_head_receipt` at the same `write_seq` where Rev-1 insertion order differed from sorted `slot` order, `bucket_head != b.bucket_head` could trigger `SelfFrozen`.
- **`S06` / `S09-F4` (`php/sync.php:295-298`):** `is_sync_url_valid()` checks `parse_url($url, PHP_URL_HOST)` for `https://` replacement URLs without rejecting embedded credentials (`PHP_URL_USER` / `PHP_URL_PASS` or `@` in authority), unlike Rust `is_valid_sync_revocation_url` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:218`).
- **`S07` / `S09-F2` (`php/lock_handler.php:477, 543`; `php/lock_rotation.php:647-650`):** `lock_handle_ticket()`, `lock_handle_batch()`, and `lock_handle_import()` enforce `max_inodes` on new bucket creation without enforcing `$cfg['max_buckets']` (currently only checked in `php/lock_rotation.php:338`).
