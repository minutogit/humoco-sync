## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S05-06` — Unused `EmailConfig.enabled` Flag (`rust/src/config.rs:215`, `config.example.toml:63`, `rust/src/mailer.rs:56-83`)**
  - **Problem:** `EmailConfig` defines `pub enabled: bool` (defaulting to `false`, and set to `false` in `config.example.toml:63` alongside `registration_gates = ["email", "code"]` and `transport = "log"`), yet no runtime path checks `email.enabled`. Even when `email.enabled = false` and `transport = "smtp"`, `Mailer::send_mail` dials SMTP and sends emails, and `/register` renders the Email Verification gate whenever `"email"` is in `registration_gates`.
  - **Options:**
    1. Enforce `cfg.email.enabled` as a hard prerequisite in `Config::gate_enabled("email")` and `Mailer::send_mail` (returning `Err("Email transport disabled")` when `false`, and aligning `config.example.toml` comments/defaults).
    2. Delete `EmailConfig.enabled` under `zero-compat` if `transport` (`log`/`sendmail`/`smtp`) + `mode.registration_gates` already express the operator's intent cleanly.
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1** if operators expect `[email] enabled = false` to suppress outbound SMTP during staging, or **Option 2** if `transport` + `registration_gates` are preferred as the single source of truth. Left untouched without changing config behavior or deleting fields.

## Cross-Slice Follow-Up Notes *(Out of `S05` Scope)*
Observed during baseline tests and cross-module inspection; left untouched for owning slices:
- **`S04` (`rust/src/ratelimit.rs:102-109`, `366-378`):** **Intermittent Baseline Test Failure Observed!** On the unmodified baseline run before any `S05` edits, `ratelimit::tests::test_s04_ratelimiter_bounded_maps_and_preserves_throttled` failed at `ratelimit.rs:375` (`Saturated /24 subnet cap must remain enforced under flood`). Root cause: `check_and_record_registration_subnet` eviction sorts by `(v.count >= limit, v.count)`. When `limit == 1`, all flooded subnets also have `(true, 1)`, so ties break by `HashMap` randomized `RandomState` order and intermittently evict `blocked_subnet_ip` (`192.0.2.10`) (~25% of runs). Adding a `last_seen_secs: u64` tie-breaker on `SubnetCounter` (matching `Bucket.last_refill_secs`) or preserving saturated entries cleanly resolves this.
- **`S08` (`rust/src/config.rs:469-634`, `rust/Cargo.toml:35`):** `Config::validate()` does not validate `email.transport` (`log|sendmail|smtp`), `email.smtp_tls_mode` (`tls|starttls|plain`), or `mode.register_path` collisions with `/`, `/admin`, `/sync`, `/sync.php`, `/anchors`, `/api` (which would panic `axum::Router` at startup). Also `reqwest` in `rust/Cargo.toml:35` is only used in `tests/integration_tests.rs` (`[dev-dependencies]` candidate).
- **`S03` (`rust/src/db.rs:1921-1951`):** `create_redeem_codes` accepts arbitrary unvalidated `custom_prefix` (could embed whitespace or control characters in codes) and silently clamps `max_uses == 0` to `1`.
