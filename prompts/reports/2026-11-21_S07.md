## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S07-07` — Unauthenticated Cold-Bucket Creation via `current_manifest` (`php/lock_rotation.php:319-345`, `388-399`, parity with `SYNC-S02-05`)**
  - **Problem:** `lock_bootstrap_anchor()` permits creating persistent bucket (`meta/buckets/<bucket>.json`), rotation (`meta/rotations/<anchor>.json`), and static anchor (`anchors/<anchor>.bin`) files from any synthetically self-signed `AnchorManifest` containing `S.lock_pub` (`GET ?info`) via `pre_freeze` (which verifies no authority signature), `heartbeat`, or `propose` without a `GenesisTicket` (even when `allow_pow_tickets = false`) and without incrementing `used_inodes`.
  - **Options:**
    1. Reject cold-bucket creation in unauthenticated `pre_freeze` (require existing bucket/rotation `404`), and enforce a dedicated `max_buckets` cap in `lock_bootstrap_anchor` alongside Rust's `bootstrap_bucket_and_rotation_if_supplied` (`SYNC-S02-05`) without altering `used_inodes` slot accounting or `SPEC.md` payloads.
    2. Require a `GenesisTicket` or PoW gate on cold-bootstrapping unseen `anchor_id`s in `?lock_freeze` (requires `SPEC.md` wire extension + Rust/PHP parity + updating cold-bootstrap tests).
    3. Accept IP rate limiting + `max_retention_quarter` quarterly GC as sufficient bound for empty bucket metadata.
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1** — block cold-bucket creation in unauthenticated `pre_freeze` and enforce a shared `max_buckets` guard identically across Rust (`SYNC-S02-05`) and PHP (`SYNC-S07-07`).
