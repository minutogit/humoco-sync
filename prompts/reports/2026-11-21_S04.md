1. **Cluster 1 — Authenticated Rate-Limit Ordering & Streaming `PUT` Validation (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs`)**
   - Removed `allow_pubkey` from unauthenticated `handle_head` (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs:212-220`) and moved `allow_pubkey(&pubkey)` strictly **after** `verify_ed25519_hex` (and before nonce consumption) in `handle_put_slot` (`609-611`), `handle_delete_slot` (`746-748`), `handle_delete_account` (`818-820`), and `handle_post_lock_ticket` (`991-1003`).
   - Updated `handle_put_slot` (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs:557-595`) to resolve `resolve_member_access` & exact per-channel byte quota (`403` if unauthorized), pre-check `Content-Length > channel_max_bytes` (`413`), stream-cap via `axum::body::to_bytes(body, channel_max_bytes)`, and reject `bytes.is_empty()` (`400 Empty payload`) before signature verification & nonce consumption (resolving the `S03` follow-up hint & `php/sync.php:1359` parity).
   - Added `optional_header_str` (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs:1467-1477`) so non-UTF-8 `X-Channel`/`X-Drop-Prefix`/`X-Media-Id` headers fail `400`, and updated `gone_revoked_response(state, true)` (`1452-1465`) to emit an empty body on `HEAD` `410`.
   - Regression test: `test_s04_auth_rate_limit_ordering_and_put_streaming_validation` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

2. **Cluster 2 — Static `.lock` Conditional `304`, `PUT ?members` `100k` Capacity & Lock-Disabled Fast-Fail (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs`)**
   - Added `if_none_match_matches` (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs:1406-1415`) supporting single, weak (`W/"..."`), comma-separated ETags, and `*` across `handle_static_anchor`, `handle_static_lock` (`1255-1272`, resolving the `S01` follow-up hint), and `handle_static_data`; restricted `touch_media_access` (`1355-1357`) to `Method::GET`; and added `static_method_not_allowed` (`1399-1404`, `Allow: GET, HEAD`).
   - Updated `handle_put_members` (`rust/src/sync_routes.rs:311-367`) to fail fast `403` when `!enable_admin_app`, deduplicated `handle_put_members` & `handle_put_revoke` via `verify_admin_request_with_body`, raised the authenticated `PUT ?members` limit to `8 MiB` (`100,000` members), and added early `!state.config.lock.enabled` -> `404` checks across lock routes (`900-1193`).
   - Regression test: `test_s04_static_lock_304_etag_members_capacity_and_fast_fail` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S04-06` — IPv6 `/128` vs `/64` Prefix Keying in `RateLimiter::allow_ip` (`rust/src/ratelimit.rs:59-62`)**
  - **Problem:** `allow_ip(ip)` keys IPv6 clients by full `/128` address, whereas IPv6 allocations assign at least a `/64` (`2^64` addresses), allowing one adversary with a `/64` to rotate `/128` addresses and evade `ip_requests_per_minute` (while `check_and_record_registration_subnet` groups IPv6 by `/48`). Conversely, grouping all IPv6 into `/64` could throttle distinct legitimate users sharing a multi-tenant hosting/campus `/64` egress.
  - **Options:**
    1. Group non-loopback IPv6 addresses by `/64` prefix in `allow_ip` (matching common ISP subscriber granularity).
    2. Make IPv6 rate-limit prefix length configurable (`[security] ipv6_rate_limit_prefix = 64` or `128`) or keep `/128` for parity with `php/sync.php` (`REMOTE_ADDR`).
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1 (or Option 2 defaulting to `/64` for public IPv6)** if public community servers expect single-subscriber IPv6 DoS resistance; left untouched (`/128`) pending maintainer confirmation.
