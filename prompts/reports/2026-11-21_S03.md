## Implemented Safe Fixes

1. **Cluster 1 — Nonce Expiry Boundary & Public Registration / Redeem Guards (`rust/src/db.rs`)**
   - `SyncDb::check_and_consume_nonce` (`rust/src/db.rs:401-419`): Tightened expiry check from `exp > now_secs` to `exp >= now_secs` and switched expiry calculation to `now_secs.saturating_add(NONCE_TTL_SECS)`.
   - `SyncDb::register_or_upgrade_user_by_hmac` (`rust/src/db.rs:1782-1812`): Rejected non-admin (`gate != "admin"`) registration attempts targeting already-registered pubkeys (`403 Account is suspended` or `409 Public key is already registered`).
   - `SyncDb::redeem_code_for_pubkey` (`rust/src/db.rs:1960-2002`): Validated existing account state prior to incrementing `used_count` — blocked `Suspended` accounts (`403`), downgrades to lower/equal-quota profiles (`409`), and timed overwrites of unlimited (`valid_until = None`) same-profile subscriptions (`409`), while keeping valid upgrades and timed renewals intact.
   - Regression test: `test_s03_nonce_boundary_and_public_account_integrity` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

2. **Cluster 2 — Hybrid Isolation, Free-Pool Classification & Quota Integrity (`rust/src/db.rs`)**
   - `is_owner_free_pool` (`rust/src/db.rs:2468-2501`) + `has_any_raw_admin_membership` (`rust/src/db.rs:514-525`): Resolved `Suspended` or gate-disabled `REGISTERED_USERS` records via `resolve_registered_profile` and `ADMIN_MEMBERS` fallback before defaulting true orphans to `true`, and reused `is_owner_free_pool` across `put_media` (`rust/src/db.rs:1100-1120`), `admin_status` (`rust/src/db.rs:1685-1715`), and `ensure_quota_with_media_eviction` (`rust/src/db.rs:2365-2450`).
   - `put_slot` & `put_media` (`rust/src/db.rs:888-890, 1015-1017`): Rejected empty payloads (`400 Empty payload`) matching `php/sync.php:1359`, replaced signed `i64` delta casts with unsigned comparisons, and converted quota/telemetry sums to `saturating_add`.
   - `put_admin_members` (`rust/src/db.rs:1447-1488`): Exempted any pubkey present in `REGISTERED_USERS` (`reg_table.get(...)?.is_some()`) from slot/media pruning regardless of `AccountStatus::Suspended`.
   - Regression test: `test_s03_suspended_pool_isolation_empty_put_and_hybrid_prune` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

3. **Cluster 3 — Capability & TTL Consistency, Panic-Free Profile Lookups & Secret Zeroization (`rust/src/db.rs`)**
   - `admin_channels_for_pubkey_read` (`rust/src/db.rs:527-545`): Skipped `"lock"` when `!cfg.lock.enabled` (matching `php/sync.php:771` and registered users at `db.rs:637, 666`).
   - `is_ttl_expired` (`rust/src/db.rs:2293-2295`), `head_capability` (`rust/src/db.rs:802-860`), `put_slot` (`rust/src/db.rs:922-949`), `put_media` (`rust/src/db.rs:1054-1087`): Aligned `HEAD` occupation/ETag and `PUT` `If-Match`/`If-None-Match: *` preconditions with `GET` TTL expiry (`get_slot_blob`/`get_media_blob` and `php/sync.php:832, 1498`), preserving `old_size` from stored bytes for quota accounting.
   - Converted direct `cfg.profiles[...]` indexing (`resolve_registered_profile`, `resolve_member_access_in_txn`, `run_maintenance`) to `.get(...).ok_or_else(|| DbError::http(500, ...))?` and wrapped `sk_bytes` / `derived_sk_bytes` / `SyncDb::pepper` with `Zeroizing` / `Drop::drop` zeroization (`rust/src/db.rs:287-291, 343, 371`).
   - Regression test: `test_s03_lock_disabled_capability_ttl_expiry_and_profile_safety` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S03-08` — Unauthorized Empty `DELETE media` Status Code (`rust/src/db.rs:1263-1319`)**
  - **Problem:** `SyncDb::delete_media` calls `resolve_member_access_in_txn` (`line 1277`) and discards the result with `let _ = ...`, returning `200 OK {"deleted": 0}` when an unauthorized pubkey owns zero media blobs. By contrast, `SyncDb::delete_slot` (`lines 1224-1244`) returns `403 Not authorized` when the caller is neither authorized nor owns the slot, and `php/sync.php:1759` returns `404 Not Found`. (`meta.owner_pubkey != pubkey` already blocks cross-user deletion with `403`.)
  - **Options:**
    1. Reject `!authorized && !owns_any_media` with `403` (aligning with `delete_slot`) or `404` (aligning with `php/sync.php:1759`, requiring `SPEC.md` clarification).
    2. Remove the unused `resolve_member_access_in_txn` call from `delete_media` and document idempotent `200 {"deleted": 0}` for authenticated non-members who own no blobs.
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1 (`403` parity with `delete_slot`)** if `SPEC.md` standardizes empty unauthorized `DELETE`, or **Option 2** if idempotent cleanup is preferred; left untouched to avoid breaking undocumented expectations.
