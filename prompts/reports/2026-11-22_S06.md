## Implemented Safe Fixes

1. **Cluster 1 — Media Membership Pruning & Single-Item `DELETE` Authorization (`php/sync.php`)**
   - **`SYNC-S06-08`** (`php/sync.php:1230-1250`): Guarded standard-slot filename pruning in `PUT ?members` with `if ($channel !== 'media')`, leaving media pruning exclusively to the owner-checked `$state['media_blobs']` loop (`lines 1251-1272`).
   - **`SYNC-S06-09`** (`php/sync.php:2033-2049`): Updated unauthorized (`!$isMember`) `DELETE` `media` ownership verification to check `$state['media_blobs'][$mediaId]['owner'] === $pubkey` when `X-Media-Id` is specified, falling back to `$ownsRequested` across `media_blobs` only for bulk `DELETE` (`$mediaId === ''`).
   - **Fail-First Tests:** `SYNC-S06-08` and `SYNC-S06-09` in `tests/run.sh` (`run_php_s06_security_regression_tests`).

2. **Cluster 2 — `PUT ?revoke` URL Credential Validation, `/sync` Self-Redirect & Clear Type Safety (`php/sync.php`)**
   - **`SYNC-S06-10`** (`php/sync.php:288-347, 1324-1334, 1394-1407`): Replaced `parse_url(..., PHP_URL_HOST)` in `is_sync_url_valid()` with strict authority/port/IPv6-bracket/loopback parsing rejecting authority `@` credentials, malformed IPv6 brackets, and invalid/out-of-range ports (`> 65535`); expanded `PUT ?revoke` self-redirect check to block both `/sync.php` and `/sync` sibling URLs alongside `read_url`; and required strict associative-object payloads and `clear === true` when clearing revocations.
   - **Fail-First Tests:** `SYNC-S06-10` assertions in `tests/run.sh` (`run_php_s06_security_regression_tests`).

3. **Cluster 3 — `PUT ?admin_config` `soft_warning_percent` Consistency & IPv6 `/64` Rate-Limit Grouping (`php/sync.php`)**
   - **`SYNC-S06-11`** (`php/sync.php:1513`): Replaced hardcoded `0.8` in `PUT ?admin_config` with `($softWarningPct / 100.0)`.
   - **`SYNC-S06-12`** (`php/sync.php:574-588, 608-610`): Added `sync_rate_limit_ip_key()` normalizing IPv4-mapped IPv6 (`::ffff:a.b.c.d` -> `a.b.c.d`) and grouping non-loopback IPv6 addresses by `/64` prefix while keeping `::1` isolated.
   - **Fail-First Tests:** `SYNC-S06-11` and `SYNC-S06-12` assertions in `tests/run.sh` (`run_php_s06_security_regression_tests`).
