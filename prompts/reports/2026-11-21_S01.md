## Implemented Safe Fixes

1. **Cluster 1 — Atomic Lock-Batch Commit & Ticket Replay Boundary (`rust/src/locks.rs`)**
   - `SyncDb::process_lock_batch` (`rust/src/locks.rs:806-858`): Moved `write_txn.commit()?` inside `Frozen` and `Committed` match arms; `OutputBranchClaimed` now explicitly drops `write_txn` so prefix writes `0..idx` roll back atomically without leaving orphan slots or stale `write_seq` / `used_inodes`. Receipt signing remains strictly after `write_txn.commit()?`.
   - `SyncDb::issue_genesis_ticket` (`rust/src/locks.rs:626-634`, `700-708`): Tightened replay expiry checks from `>` to `>=` and switched nonce TTL additions to `saturating_add`.
   - Regression tests: `test_s01_output_branch_claimed_aborts_partial_batch_atomically`, `test_s01_ticket_replay_rejected_at_exact_expiry_boundary` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

2. **Cluster 2 — Overflow-Safe Timestamp Windows & Strict Ed25519 Verification (`rust/src/crypto.rs`, `rust/src/locks.rs`)**
   - `verify_ed25519_hex` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:314-334`): Switched from `vk.verify` to `vk.verify_strict` (and removed unused `Verifier` import).
   - `check_timestamp_window` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:336-338`): Replaced `(now_secs as i64 - timestamp).abs()` with `(now_secs as i128).abs_diff(timestamp as i128) <= TIMESTAMP_WINDOW_SECS as u128`.
   - `issue_genesis_ticket` PoW drift check (`rust/src/locks.rs:33`, `678`): Switched `TICKET_POW_WINDOW_SECS` to `u64` and used `now_secs.abs_diff(pow_ts) > TICKET_POW_WINDOW_SECS`.
   - Regression tests: `crypto::tests::test_s01_check_timestamp_window_extreme_values_no_overflow_or_bypass`, `crypto::tests::test_s01_verify_ed25519_hex_rejects_small_order_identity_forgery`, `locks::tests::test_s01_ticket_pow_extreme_timestamp_no_panic_or_bypass`.

3. **Cluster 3 — Strict Email & Revocation-Notice Canonical Validation (`rust/src/crypto.rs`)**
   - `verify_revocation_notice` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:280-312`): Enforced exact lowercase 64-hex (`is_hex_exact`) without whitespace on `notice.drop_pub` and `replacement.drop_pub`, rejected all `char::is_control()` in `reason`, and validated `replacement.write_url` / `replacement.read_url` via `is_valid_lock_delegation_url`.
   - `canonicalize_email` (`rust/src/crypto.rs:342-411`): Enforced RFC-5322 unquoted local-part `dot-atom` bounds (`1..=64` bytes, no leading/trailing/consecutive dots) and per-label DNS hostname checks (`1..=63` bytes, no leading/trailing `-`).
   - Regression tests: `crypto::tests::test_s01_canonicalize_email_rejects_control_and_smtp_delimiters`, `crypto::tests::test_s01_verify_revocation_notice_rejects_delimiter_injection_and_bad_urls`.
