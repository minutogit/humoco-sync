## Implemented Safe Fixes

1. **Cluster 1 — Sticky `SelfFrozen` Preservation Across Rotation (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs`)**
   - Added `SelfFrozenOrigin`, `AnchorRotationRecord::self_frozen_origin` (`#[serde(default)]`), and `restore_non_proposal_freeze_state` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:87-125`).
   - Updated `Propose` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:747-756`) to capture `SelfFrozen { frozen_at, reason }` before entering `PermanentProposal`, `Veto` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:856-861`) to restore `SelfFrozen` instead of `Active`, and `Finalize` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:1389-1396`) to restore `SelfFrozen` when `in_new_committee == true`.
   - Regression test: `test_s02_self_frozen_persists_across_propose_veto_and_finalize` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

2. **Cluster 2 — Monotonic Revision Guards, Historical Committee Binding & Epoch-State Integrity (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs`)**
   - `Propose` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:664-666`): Enforced `proposal.from_rev == rot.active_manifest.revision` (`409 Manifest revision mismatch`) prior to checking `voted_proposals_by_rev`.
   - `Veto` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:823-828`): Rejected `EpochFrozen` (`409 epoch_frozen`) and `veto.from_rev != rot.active_manifest.revision` (`409 Manifest revision mismatch`).
   - `process_lock_import` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:969-978, 1076-1088`): Rejected `req.proposal.to_rev <= existing.active_manifest.revision` (`409 Target revision has already been finalized`), and preserved `BucketFreezeState::EpochFrozen` until `Finalize`.
   - `Finalize` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:1267-1279, 1316-1361`): Enforced `chain_report.historical_committees.iter().any(|c| c.contains(&lock_pub))` (`400`), rejected `new_manifest.revision < existing.active_manifest.revision` (`409`) and conflicting same-revision manifests (`409`), and returned idempotent `200` on matching same-revision retries.
   - Regression test: `test_s02_rotation_guards_block_stale_replay_downgrade_and_unrelated_finalize` (`rust/tests/integration_tests.rs`).

## Deferred Uncertainties / Design Questions

- **`SYNC-S02-05` — Unauthenticated Cold-Bucket Creation via `supplied_manifest` (`rust/src/lock_rotation.rs:260-362`, `488-583`)**
  - **Problem:** `bootstrap_bucket_and_rotation_if_supplied` permits creating persistent `LOCK_BUCKETS`, `LOCK_QUARTER_INDEX`, `LOCK_ROTATIONS`, and `LOCK_ANCHORS` records from any synthetically self-signed `AnchorManifest` containing `S.lock_pub` (`GET ?info`) via `PreFreeze` (which verifies no signature), `Heartbeat`, or `Propose` without a `GenesisTicket` (even when `allow_pow_tickets = false`) and without incrementing `used_inodes`.
  - **Options:**
    1. Reject cold-bucket creation in unauthenticated `PreFreeze` (require existing bucket/rotation `404`), and enforce a dedicated `max_buckets` / bucket-entry cap in `bootstrap_bucket_and_rotation_if_supplied` without altering `used_inodes` slot accounting or `SPEC.md` payloads.
    2. Require a `GenesisTicket` or PoW gate on cold-bootstrapping unseen `anchor_id`s in `?lock_freeze` (requires `SPEC.md` wire extension + PHP parity + updating existing cold-bootstrap tests).
    3. Accept IP rate limiting + `max_retention_quarter` quarterly GC as sufficient bound for empty bucket metadata.
  - **Recommendation:** **Option 1** — block cold-bucket creation in unauthenticated `PreFreeze` and bound empty rotation/bucket records with a separate `max_buckets` guard (preserving cold-server `Propose`/`Heartbeat` recovery without changing wire schemas).
